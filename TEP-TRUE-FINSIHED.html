The Eagler Project (TEP)

Play Eaglercraft clients right in your browser. Pick a client and press Launch; each one is only downloaded when you start it.

Warning: prefers the lightest build of each client, renders the game at a reduced resolution () and caps FPS at . Picture is blurrier and less smooth. Please also lower render distance (2-6 chunks) and turn Fancy graphics off in the game's own Video Settings: the launcher cannot change those for you.

Worlds and settings are saved in your browser for this site’s address, so keep using the same address. Clearing site data deletes them.

TEP is an unofficial, fan-made launcher and isn’t affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft or the authors of any client. Each client belongs to its respective authors; the source is listed on every card.
